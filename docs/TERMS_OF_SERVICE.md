# CampusRide Terms of Service (Draft)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **DISCLAIMER**: This document is a draft agreement prepared for diligence and demonstration purposes. It requires formal legal review by qualified counsel prior to binding enforcement.

**Effective Date:** September 21, 2026

## 1. Eligibility & University Verification
Use of CampusRide is strictly restricted to currently enrolled students, faculty, and authorized administrative staff of partner universities holding a valid institutional `.edu` email address.

## 2. Non-Commercial Peer Carpooling (Zero Payments Invariant)
1. **Cost-Sharing Only**: CampusRide is a peer carpool coordination tool designed solely for shared personal travel and mutual expense splitting.
2. **No Commercial Transit**: Drivers agree that they are not operating a taxi, livery, or for-hire commercial transport service. Recommended ride contributions reflect real vehicle running costs (fuel, tolls) and may not exceed reasonable shared costs.
3. **No In-App Payment Gateway**: The platform does not collect fares, charge passenger commissions, or disburse driver wages.

## 3. Safety & Code of Conduct
All users agree to adhere to university student honor codes and community standards:
- Drivers must maintain a valid driver's license, active vehicle registration, and mandatory state/national motor vehicle insurance.
- Passengers and drivers agree to verify trip pickups via the cryptographic 6-digit OTP or digital QR boarding pass prior to vehicle departure.
- Zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, intoxication, or reckless driving. Violations result in permanent suspension and report to the Dean of Students.

## 4. Limitation of Liability
CampusRide facilitates communication between verified students but does not own vehicles, employ drivers, or supervise transit. To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, CampusRide is not liable for accidents, route delays, or lost personal property.
